# GleamPro.ca Update Plan
*Goal: turn the site from a generalist brochure into the commercial-specialist proof machine that backs up every drop-in, email, and walkthrough. Work it in order — each phase is shippable on its own.*

**Note to developer:** this is a copy/content update, not a redesign — keep the existing layout, styling, and walkthrough form flow. All replacement copy is provided verbatim in quotes below; use it as written. Phases are ordered by priority and each is independently shippable. Questions about intent → owner decides, copy stays as written unless factually wrong.

---

## Phase 1 — Honesty & alignment fixes (≈1 hour, do first)

- [ ] **Replace "Est. 2019" everywhere it appears (homepage, footer)** with the precise version — the owners have cleaned professionally since 2019; the corporation dates from 2024, so the site must not imply the company itself is from 2019:
  > "On the floor since 2019 · Incorporated 2024 · 10 commercial sites serviced nightly"

  Short version where space is tight (e.g., footer):
  > "7 years of professional cleaning · Owner-operated every night"
- [ ] **Change "quote within 48 hours" → "written quote within 24 hours"** (walkthrough section + form disclosure).
- [ ] **Add the trial line directly beside/under the walkthrough form and the callback form:**
  > "Start with a 30-day trial. No lock-in, cancel anytime."
- [ ] **Retire "Shine · Elevate · Prestige"** motto (fluff, says nothing to a facilities buyer).
- [ ] Check phone handling: (778) 681-0922 must ring someone or hit a voicemail that says "leave your address — we'll confirm your walkthrough within one business day."

## Phase 2 — Commercial-first repositioning (≈1 evening)

- [ ] **New homepage headline (pick one):**
  1. "Your space, ready before you open. Same crew every night, owner on site."
  2. "Commercial cleaning run by the owners who do the work — nightly across Metro Vancouver."
  3. "We clean breweries, kitchens, clinics and offices every night. Yours could open spotless tomorrow."
- [ ] **New subheadline:**
  > "Family-owned commercial cleaning. Free 15-minute walkthrough, written quote in 24 hours, 30-day no-lock-in trial."
- [ ] **Demote residential:** remove the residential menu from primary nav; keep one "Residential" link in the footer pointing to a single combined page. Homepage becomes 100% commercial.
- [ ] **Trust bar under the hero (one row, small icons):**
  > $1M commercial liability insured · WorkSafeBC registered *(add only once coverage is active)* · Owner-led night crews · Nightly service, 7 days
- [ ] **Trim service areas:** lead with Burnaby, New Westminster, Vancouver ("our nightly routes"); list the rest as "also serving."

## Phase 3 — The two niche landing pages (≈1 evening each)

These are the landing pages for Gaban emails and the QR/link on the one-pager.

- [ ] **/breweries — "Brewery & Taproom Cleaning"**
  - Hero: "We clean a Vancouver brewery every single night. Taproom floors, back-of-house, washrooms — open-ready by morning."
  - Body: sticky-floor/patio-season pain, night service = zero disruption, same crew every visit, owner's cell number.
  - CTA: walkthrough form (business type pre-set to Brewery/Taproom — add the option to the dropdown).
- [ ] **/clinics — "Clinic & Medical Office Cleaning"**
  - Hero: "Daily cleaning for clinics that get inspected — because we already do it every day."
  - Body: hygiene standards, disinfection protocol, discretion, insured + WorkSafeBC.
  - CTA: same walkthrough form (add Clinic/Medical to the dropdown).
- [ ] Add both to the Commercial nav menu.

## Phase 4 — Proof (ongoing, start this week)

- [ ] **Google Business Profile:** claim/update it, category "Commercial cleaning service," New Westminster. This will win more walkthroughs than the website itself.
- [ ] **Ask every friendly site contact for a Google review** — target 10 in 60 days. Script:
  > "We're growing the business this year — a one-line Google review from you would mean a lot. Here's the direct link."
- [ ] **Replace the anonymous testimonial** with at least one named one (first name + business type is enough: "Mike, Taproom Manager, East Vancouver"). Ask permission explicitly.
- [ ] **Photos:** 5–6 real photos — the two of you, the crew, before/afters of a kitchen line and taproom floor (get client OK). Real photos beat stock everywhere on the site.

## Phase 5 — Measure (30 min, once)

- [ ] Install basic analytics (GA4 or a simple privacy-friendly counter) — watch one number only: **walkthrough form submissions per week**.
- [ ] Make sure form submissions email you instantly AND log somewhere (sheet/inbox label) — a lost lead is a lost contract.
- [ ] Add UTM links for Gaban emails (`?utm_source=gaban`) so you know which outreach produced which walkthrough.
