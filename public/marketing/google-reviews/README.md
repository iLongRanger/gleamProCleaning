## Files

- `google-review-qr.svg`: scalable QR code for professional printing.

## Before printing

1. Scan the QR code with both an iPhone and an Android phone.
2. Confirm it opens Gleam Pro's Google review form.
3. Print at 100% scale. Do not crop the white margin around the QR code.

Ask for honest feedback from genuine customers. Do not offer discounts, gifts, or other incentives in exchange for a review.
